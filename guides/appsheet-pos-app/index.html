How to make your own POS in AppSheet

Build your own point of sale on Google AppSheet: products, a checkout form with line items, automatic totals, live stock, daily reports and PDF receipts. Watch the video, then follow the written steps.

By Joe Tayactac, AppSheet developer with 100+ apps built

What you'll build

  • A product catalog with prices, photos and barcodes
  • A checkout form where cashiers add items to a sale
  • Automatic line totals, sale totals and live stock
  • Today's sales by payment method for closing the till
  • PDF receipts and cashier permissions

1Set up your Google Sheet

A POS needs three tabs. The split between Sales and Sale Items is the backbone: one sale has many line items, the same way a receipt has one header and many lines. Put column names in row 1 and keep the sheet clean.

Tab 1: Products

Product ID, Name, Category, Price, Barcode, Photo, Starting Stock

Tab 2: Sales

Sale ID, Date, Cashier, Payment Method, Customer Email, Send Receipt

Tab 3: Sale Items

Item ID, Sale, Product, Qty, Unit Price

2Create the app from the sheet

  1. In your Google Sheet, open Extensions and choose AppSheet, then Create an app.
  2. In the AppSheet editor, open Data and add the Sales and Sale Items tabs as tables.
  3. Allow adds and updates on Sales and Sale Items. Products can stay read-only for cashiers.

3Set the column types

Products

ColumnTypeSettings
Product IDTextKey, initial value UNIQUEID(), hidden. Or use Barcode as the key (see step 5).
NameTextLabel.
PricePriceThe current selling price.
BarcodeTextScannable.
PhotoImageMakes the product picker much faster at the counter.
Starting StockNumberStock on hand when you start using the app.

Sales

ColumnTypeSettings
Sale IDTextKey, initial value UNIQUEID(), hidden.
DateDateTimeInitial value NOW().
CashierEmailInitial value USEREMAIL(), so every sale records who rang it up.
Payment MethodEnumCash, Card, Mobile, or whatever you accept.
Customer EmailEmailOptional, for emailed receipts.
Send ReceiptYes/NoUsed by the receipt button in step 7. Hide it from the form.

Sale Items

ColumnTypeSettings
Item IDTextKey, initial value UNIQUEID(), hidden.
SaleRefSource table: Sales. Turn on Is a part of.
ProductRefSource table: Products. Required.
QtyNumberInitial value 1.
Unit PricePriceInitial value [Product].[Price]. A real column, not a formula.
Is a part of makes each sale own its line items: in the Sales form, the cashier adds items right inside the sale, and deleting a sale also deletes its items.
Why Unit Price is a stored column: it copies the price at the moment of the sale. If you raise a price next month, last month's sales and receipts keep the price the customer actually paid. Never make it an App formula.

4Add totals and live stock

Add these virtual columns. AppSheet keeps them up to date for you.

In Sale Items: Line Total (Price)

App formula: Line Total
[Qty] * [Unit Price]

In Sales: Total (Price) and Item Count (Number)

App formula: Total
SUM([Related Sale Items][Line Total])
App formula: Item Count
SUM([Related Sale Items][Qty])

In Products: Units Sold and Stock Left (Number)

App formula: Units Sold
SUM([Related Sale Items][Qty])
App formula: Stock Left
[Starting Stock] - [Units Sold]

Because stock is calculated from sales, there's no bot to subtract quantities and nothing to get out of sync. When you receive new stock, add it with a Stock Movements table, as in the inventory guide.

5Build the checkout flow

  1. Create a Form view on Sales called New Sale. Because of Is a part of, the form shows a Sale Items section where the cashier adds products and quantities.
  2. Show Payment Method and Customer Email in the form. Hide Sale ID, Date, Cashier and Send Receipt; they fill themselves.
  3. Create a Deck or Table view of Sales, newest first, so cashiers can open a past sale and see its Total and items.

Barcode scanning: if every product has a unique barcode, make Barcode the key of Products and turn on Scannable for the Product column in Sale Items. The cashier taps the scan icon and the product is picked. Decide this before entering real data, because changing a key later breaks existing links.

6See daily sales at a glance

Create a slice on Sales for today's transactions:

Slice row filter: Today
DATE([Date]) = TODAY()

Show it in a Table view grouped by Payment Method, with the group aggregate set to the sum of Total. You get today's cash, card and mobile totals for closing the till. Because every sale lives in your Google Sheet, monthly reports and pivot tables are a few clicks away too.

7Send receipts as PDFs

  1. Create a Google Doc as the receipt template. Use AppSheet's template tags for the fields, and a Start and End block for the line items, like this:
Receipt template (Google Doc)
Receipt <<[Sale ID]>>
<<[Date]>>

<<Start: [Related Sale Items]>>
<<[Product].[Name]>>  x<<[Qty]>>  <<[Line Total]>>
<<End>>

Total: <<[Total]>>
Paid by: <<[Payment Method]>>
  1. Add an action on Sales named Send receipt that sets Send Receipt to TRUE, and show it as a button on the sale.
  2. Create a bot for Sales on Updates, with the condition below. Its task creates a PDF from the template and emails it to [Customer Email], or saves it to Drive for printing.
Bot condition
[Send Receipt] = TRUE
Don't trigger receipts on Adds. A sale and its line items are saved together, and a bot that fires on the new sale can run before the items are written. The receipt then comes out with missing lines. Pressing a button after the sale is saved avoids this completely.

8Set up cashiers and permissions

  1. Share the app with each cashier's Google account.
  2. To let cashiers see only their own sales while managers see everything, add a security filter on Sales:
Security filter: Sales
OR(USERROLE() = "Admin", [Cashier] = USEREMAIL())

Test with a cashier account before you go live, and keep Products editable only for managers.

Common issues

Stuck on something else? Use the Troubleshoot button at the top and tell me what's happening.

The sale total shows 0

Totals are virtual columns, so they appear once the line items are saved. Check that the Sale column in Sale Items is a Ref to Sales with Is a part of turned on, and that Line Total exists in Sale Items.

Old sales changed when I updated a price

Unit Price is probably an App formula. Make it a normal column with the Initial value [Product].[Price], so each line keeps the price from the moment of the sale.

The receipt PDF is missing items

The receipt bot is most likely triggered when a sale is added, which can run before the items are saved. Use the Send receipt button and a bot on Updates instead, as shown in step 7.

Scanning doesn't pick the product

Scanning fills the Ref with the barcode text, so it only works when Barcode is the key of Products and the Product column in Sale Items is Scannable.

Is AppSheet a good fit for a busy counter?

It works well when you need a POS tailored to an unusual workflow. Because it syncs through Google Sheets, it can feel slower than dedicated POS software during busy periods. If you just need fast checkout, a ready-made POS like GoOnlinePOS.com may save you the build.
