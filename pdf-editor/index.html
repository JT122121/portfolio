What you can do

A focused editor for correcting PDFs: fix a typo, update a date or amount, add a note, or cover something on a scanned page.

Edit existing text

Click any line in the PDF and type. The size, colour and position of the original are kept.

Keep the original font

Uses the fonts installed on your computer to match the document, with bold, italic and underline.

Add text, photos and signatures

Place a note, a date, a logo or a picture of your signature anywhere on the page, then drag and resize it.

Fix scanned PDFs

White out words on a scan in the paper's own colour, then type the correction on top.

Works offline

After the first visit it opens without a connection. Install it from Chrome or Edge to use it like a desktop app.

Private by design

Your file is processed on your device. There is no server to upload to, no account and no tracking.

How to edit a PDF

Three steps, and the file stays on your computer the whole time.

  1. Open your PDF

    Choose a file or drop it onto this page. It opens right here in the browser.

  2. Click and type

    Click existing text to change it, add new text or an image, or use Whiteout to cover part of a scan.

  3. Download

    Save the edited copy. Your original file is left untouched.

Your documents never leave your device

Most online PDF editors upload your file to their servers. This one doesn't. The editing engine is downloaded to your browser and does all the work locally, so invoices, contracts and forms stay with you.

We don't save your files, keep copies, or collect what you type. When you close the tab, your document is gone from this site. Only the edited file you download remains, on your own computer.

You can check it yourself: open a PDF, switch off your Wi-Fi, and keep editing.

Not for redaction. Edited and whited-out text is covered, not deleted, and can still be copied out of the downloaded file. To permanently remove confidential information, use a dedicated redaction tool.

Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The editor runs entirely in your browser. Your PDF is opened, edited and saved on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

Do you save my files or data?

No. We don't store your PDFs, images or anything you type, and there is no account. Everything is processed in your browser and disappears when you close the tab. Fonts you choose to add are kept only in your own browser so they're ready next time.

Can I edit the text in a PDF in the same font?

Yes, when the font is installed on your computer. In Chrome or Edge, click Use this computer's fonts and the editor matches the PDF's fonts automatically. Otherwise it uses a close stand-in, or you can add the font file.

Can I edit a scanned PDF?

A scan is a picture, so there is no text to click. Use Whiteout to cover the words you want to change, then Add text to type the new words on top.

Does the PDF editor work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the editor is stored by your browser and opens without an internet connection. In Chrome and Edge you can also install it as an app.

Can I add a photo, logo or signature to a PDF?

Yes. Click Image, or paste or drop a picture onto the page, then drag to move it and drag the corner to resize. A signature saved as a PNG with a transparent background looks best. This places a picture of your signature; it is not a certificate-based digital signature.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no page limit.

Can I use it to remove sensitive information?

No. Edited and whited-out text is covered, not deleted, and can still be copied from the file. Use a dedicated redaction tool for confidential information.
