Fill in the Bahrain Vehicle Licensing form online

The Ministry of Interior's General Directorate of Traffic form for registration, renewal, transfer of ownership and other vehicle services. Type your details into the boxes, then download the filled PDF to print or send.

Services covered by this form

  • Register a new vehicle
  • Public transport licence
  • Vehicle annual registration
  • Change vehicle plates
  • Transfer of ownership
  • Vehicle cancellation
  • Replacement copy
  • Issue certificates
  • Reserve a number for later use
  • Other services

How to fill it in

  1. Open the form with the button above. It opens in the editor in your browser, ready to type.
  2. Pick the service. Click the box next to the service you need and type X.
  3. Fill in the vehicle owner's details: name, CPR or CR number, nationality, address, phone and email. For the CPR boxes, type the number and increase Spacing in the sidebar until each digit sits in its own box.
  4. For a transfer of ownership, also fill in the buyer's details.
  5. Add the vehicle details (make, model, chassis and engine number, colour, year) and the insurance details.
  6. Sign. Print and sign by hand, or use Image in the editor to place a picture of your signature.
  7. Leave the official sections blank, including "For Official Use" and page 2, which is the inspector's technical inspection sheet.
  8. Download the filled PDF.

Notes printed on the form

Transfer the certificate of insurance before the transfer of ownership.

Vehicle registration must be done within 3 months from the date of inspection, and seals are valid for 2 weeks only.

Independent tool. This site is not affiliated with the Ministry of Interior or the General Directorate of Traffic. Requirements and form versions can change, so confirm with the Vehicle Licensing Division that this is the version they currently accept.

Questions

Can I fill in the form online?

Yes. Open it in the editor, click inside each box and type, then download the filled PDF to print or send. Your details are not uploaded anywhere.

How do I tick a box?

Click inside the box and type a capital X.

Can I type in Arabic?

Fill the form in English. The editor does not lay out Arabic text correctly.

Do I need to fill in page 2?

Page 2 is the technical inspection sheet, which is completed by the inspector. Leave it blank unless you are told otherwise.

Is this the official website of the Ministry of Interior?

No. This is an independent tool for filling in the form. Check with the General Directorate of Traffic that this version of the form is the one currently accepted.
