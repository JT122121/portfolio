How I work

Building an app isn't just screens and buttons

It starts with understanding the work, and it doesn't stop at launch.

  1. 1

    Understand the workflow

    We walk through how the work actually happens today: who enters what, where it gets stuck, and what you need to see.

  2. 2

    Build and test with your team

    I build the app on your existing data, your team tries it on real work, and I fix what doesn't fit.

  3. 3

    Keep improving

    Once real people use it, new needs show up. I keep refining the app as your business changes.

About me

From Excel to AppSheet expert

I didn't start as a traditional programmer. During COVID, the company I worked for ran its daily operations on Excel, and as the processes got more complicated I kept thinking there had to be a better way.

That question led me to AppSheet. I learned by building: create an app, test it, fix what needs fixing, then build the next feature. More than five years later, I build real applications for clients and run my own products, like GoOnlinePOS.com.

You can build an application around the way a business actually works.
